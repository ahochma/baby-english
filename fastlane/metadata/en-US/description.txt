Your child's first English words, with a clear, friendly voice, big happy pictures and Pip, a cheerful little chick who plays along.

Made for ages 3 to 6. No reading needed. No ads. No tracking.

THREE WAYS TO PLAY
• Flashcards: tap anywhere to see and hear the next word.
• Bubbles: pop floating bubbles to hear each word. Every touch does something, so there's no way to fail.
• Find it!: hear a word and find the picture. It starts with 2 big pictures and grows with your child. Pip cheers every right answer.

HEBREW → ENGLISH MODE
For Hebrew-speaking families: hear the Hebrew word first, then the English one ("כלב… Dog!"). Switch it on or off in the Parent Zone.

BUILT FOR LITTLE HANDS
• Big, chunky buttons that press down like real toys
• Quick taps and palm touches don't skip ahead or exit by accident
• Games close only when a grown-up holds the house button
• Sticker rewards, happy celebrations and a daily surprise gift from Pip

FOR PARENTS
• See which words your child has heard
• Works offline and on silent mode
• Everything grown-up sits behind a parental gate

FREE TO TRY
The Animals world and every game are free forever. One small, one-time purchase unlocks five more worlds: Vehicles, Food, Colors, My Body and Numbers (60 words in all). No subscription. Family Sharing included.
