# Game ideas for later versions

Families love the games where you **hear a word, look and touch**: Find it! and Bubble Pop. Feed Pip and Peekaboo shipped in the next version on that model. The ideas below are saved for later versions. They share the same rules:

- one spoken sentence per prompt (recorded whole, never stitched)
- a big target, no reading and no time pressure
- a wrong touch never punishes: it teaches a word or wiggles
- 5 rounds → a sticker
- calm mode keeps it gentle
- the same build on iOS and Android

| # | Game | How it plays | Best world | New voice lines |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Balloons** | Coloured balloons float up. Pip says "Pop the blue balloon!" | Colors | `pop_<color>` (EN + HE) |
| 4 | **Fishing** | Fish carrying pictures swim by. "Catch the duck!" The child taps (or drags a rod to) the right fish. Moving targets, so a step harder. | Animals, then all | `catch_<id>` (EN + HE) |
| 5 | **Sorting train** | Train carriages in 2–3 colours. The child drags each item into the carriage of its colour, hearing the word each time. Teaches colours and sorting. | Colors + any world | `this_goes_<color>` or reuse word clips |
| 6 | **Shadows** | Match a picture to its silhouette. Calm and slow, a natural fit for calm mode and bedtime. | All | reuse word clips |

**Suggested order:** Balloons (it reuses the Bubble Pop engine), then Shadows (no new voice), then Fishing, then the Sorting train.
