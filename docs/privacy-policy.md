# Pip English — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 2026_

**The short version: Pip English collects no personal data. None.**

## What we collect
Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics SDKs, no tracking and no third-party code. It never connects to our servers, because there are none.

## What is stored on the device
To keep your child's progress, the app stores these on the device only:
- stickers earned, and the day the last daily gift was opened
- which words have been heard, and how Find it! answers went (so the app can repeat the words your child finds hard)
- the daily play streak
- your settings (sound effects, music, calm mode, Hebrew→English)

This data never leaves the device. Deleting the app deletes it.

## Purchases
The optional "Unlock Everything" purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, email address or payment details.

## Children
Pip English is made for children under 5 and is designed to comply with COPPA (US) and GDPR-K (EU/UK). We do not collect personal information from children. Purchases, settings and external links sit behind a parental gate.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

## Contact
Questions? Contact us via the support link on the Pip English App Store page.
